My Accounts

Ek customer ke kai account ho sakte hain. Active account header ke dropdown se ya neeche "Use this" se badla jaata hai.

Add-on Services (Decorator pattern)

Active account par extra features lagao ya hatao. Har feature ek wrapper class hai jo account ke upar chadh jaata hai — kitne bhi ek saath laga sakte ho, aur account class ka code bilkul nahi badalta.

Interest Calculator (Strategy pattern)

Har account type par interest ka formula alag hai. Formula account ke andar nahi, alag strategy class me rakha hai.

Open a New Account

Factory Method — type chuno, uske rules apne aap lag jaayenge.
