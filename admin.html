All Accounts (State pattern — freeze / close)

Account ka status badalte hi uska behaviour badal jaata hai. Frozen account me paisa aa sakta hai lekin ja nahi sakta, Closed account me kuch bhi nahi hota. Close karne par bacha hua balance customer ko de diya jaata hai aur uski entry banti hai.

All Transactions (Command pattern — reverse)

Poore bank ki transactions. Reverse karne par balance wapas theek hota hai, original row par REVERSED lagta hai aur nayi reversal entry banti hai. Transfer reverse karo to paane wale ka paisa bhi wapas aata hai, aur cashback bhi wapas jaata hai.
